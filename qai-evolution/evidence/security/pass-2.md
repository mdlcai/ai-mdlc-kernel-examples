## Areas re-checked with no new finding

- **Route sweep.** All 17 route modules were checked for authn and tenant scope.
  - Every non-public handler depends on `get_principal`, through `require_role`, `require_session*`, `UserAdmin`, `Session*` or `require_member_session`.
  - The public handlers are signup, login, password-reset, invite accept, health and live. They are rate limited, except health (P2-5).
  - Scope always comes from `principal.tenant_id`.
  - Foreign ids return 404.
  - Child creates verify the parent within the tenant.
  - `switch-tenant` checks membership.
  - The API-key role is capped at the creator's live role.
  - Member PATCH and DELETE enforce the owner rules and the last-owner lock.
  - The invite and API-key role ceilings hold.
- **Sessions.** The membership is re-read on every request, so a removal or demotion takes effect immediately. The CSRF and Origin checks apply to every unsafe cookie-authenticated method.
- **Governance.** Self-approval is blocked. Approval needs `is_reviewer` and a session. Release needs a session admin. Evidence is re-assessed at approve and at release.
