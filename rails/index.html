Security audit: what was found

Findings from the audit and the reviewer's checks of the fixes. Remediated items were fixed in the build; deferred items are accepted with the reason recorded.

IDSeverityFindingDisposition
SA-1HighLogin and password-reset throttles compared the raw path, so /login/, /login.json and form-encoded bodies skipped the per-IP and per-email limits.Remediated throttles key on the normalised route and the parsed parameters.
SA-2MediumOutbound webhooks had no overall deadline, so a slow endpoint could hold the shared delivery sweep.Remediated 5 s deadline per attempt; slow endpoints are deferred.
SA-3MediumOpen redirect after sign-in through a tab character in the return path.Remediated control characters, backslashes and dot-segment tricks refused.

SA-4Lowbcrypt ignores bytes after 72, so very long passphrases are only as strong as their first 72 bytes.Deferred needs a versioned pre-hash migration with its own ADR.
N3, N4LowOne endpoint with many due retries can delay other tenants' webhook retries; two narrow idempotent-resume edges around processor failover.Residual scheduled after 1.0.1; failover across two live accounts is not reachable in contract mode.

Result after the re-scan: 0 critical, 0 high, 0 medium open; 1 low deferred and 2 low residual, each with a recorded reason. Dependency audits and Brakeman report nothing; Semgrep's 8 hits are false positives.

Open items

  • Load test: sustained latency targets are missed on a single local host. No errors and no duplicate charges under load.
  • Live Stripe payments were not verified; Stripe runs in contract mode until keys are provisioned.
  • HSTS is absent on the localhost edge, because the local certificate is not trusted by the host store (ADR-048).
  • Low security residuals SA-4, N3 and N4, and three reviewer advisories (ADR-055).
  • After 1.0.1, work continued on agent mandates (spend-capped, expiring authority an AI agent can use to pay). That work has not yet been through the ship gates, so it is not counted on this page.
