BookFlow

A bookings and approvals portal for rooms, equipment and services. Employees request a resource for a time window, approval rules set by the organisation decide who signs off, and the database itself refuses any overlapping booking on the same resource. Every change is written to an append-only audit log. This page records what the build produced, what passed, what was fixed on the way, and what is still open.

Open items

  • F-2 (medium, build-time only) is deferred until a stable Next.js release bundles a patched postcss.
  • Real email delivery through Resend was not exercised. The build used the stub transport by design.
  • TLS termination, encryption at rest and the backup schedule with point-in-time recovery are infrastructure responsibilities. The build documents them but does not verify them from inside the app.
  • Cloud deployment was not performed. The upgrade path is documented in the build's QUICKSTART.md.
  • The manual smoke checklist (SMOKE-TEST.md) has no recorded results in the evidence files.
