SmartCoder

SmartCoder is a training and quality-assurance tool for medical coding teams, and it runs only on synthetic patient encounters. Coders work a queue and assign ICD-10-CM diagnosis codes and procedure codes entered by code. A validation engine checks each chart for format, sequencing and code-combination problems. Supervisors audit submitted charts and return them with feedback, and managers watch a live queue dashboard. This page records what the build produced, which gates passed, which findings were fixed or accepted, and what is still open.

Result after the narrative audit: 0 critical, 0 high and 0 medium items open. DEP-1 is accepted with rationale. The secret scan and the SQL injection sweep are clean, and every query is parameterized. The narrative audit lists one low item deferred (SEC-04). The inline gate also tracked the register residual.

Open items

  • Encryption and secrets are delegated, not built in. Chart payloads (PHI-equivalent JSONB) are stored as plaintext in Postgres, and at-rest encryption relies on the host disk (SEC-12). The secrets loader falls back to environment variables locally, and AWS Secrets Manager is not wired in production (SEC-11). The data is Synthea synthetic.
  • External services are placeholder-gated and not provisioned: Resend, Datadog, Sentry and AWS Secrets Manager. Email stays queued in the outbox, so email delivery is not verified.
  • No measured results are recorded for the SPEC §9 performance budgets: chart load under 2 s, audit submission under 500 ms and queue refresh under 1 s.
  • The manual smoke checklist has no recorded result for each of its 18 checks.
  • CPT is out of v1, and procedure codes are entered by code only. CPT licensing must be resolved before any CPT descriptors ship (RESEARCH §6).
  • The annual ICD-10-CM refresh process is not formalized, and code-set pinning per scenario is a post-v1 item (RESEARCH §6).
