The platform

Full-Spectrum Security Intelligence

Six disciplines, one correlated view of organizational risk.

◆

Continuous Discovery

Diff analysis across scans surfaces new ports, services, and exposures the moment they appear on your attack surface.

◆

AI-Assisted Pentesting

A multi-agent engine reasons through attack paths and validates findings — with human approval gates before any state-changing action.

◆

Vulnerability Assessment

A template-driven signature engine plus safe recon (DNS, HTTP, TLS) correlates findings to assets, CVSS, and CWE.

◆

OSINT & Threat Intel

Correlate indicators — domain, IP, hash, email — against your assets and findings, enriched with STIX threat feeds.

◆

Compliance Mapping

Automatically map findings to SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria and PCI DSS v4.0. Generate audit-ready evidence.

◆

Continuous Retesting

Track remediation, schedule retests, and revalidate findings — with a tamper-evident audit trail for every action.

Workflow

From Target to Intelligence

01

Add & Authorize

Register a domain, IP range, or app and prove ownership. Deny-by-default scope keeps every scan in-bounds.

02

Scan & Correlate

Safe recon and signature checks run in real time, streaming findings correlated to assets and threat intel.

03

Validate & Prioritize

AI reasons about exploitability and attack paths; risk-scored findings rise to the top of the queue.

04

Remediate & Prove

Guide fixes, retest continuously, and export compliance evidence auditors accept.
