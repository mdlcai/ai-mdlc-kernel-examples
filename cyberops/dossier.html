CyberOps

A multi-tenant security operations platform for teams that test infrastructure they own. Targets must be verified before any scan may touch them. The platform runs safe reconnaissance, correlates CVSS and CWE tagged findings, plans AI-assisted pentests that stop at a human approval gate, investigates indicators, maps findings to SOC 2, PCI DSS and OWASP controls, and exports PDF, JSON and CSV reports. This page records what the build produced, what passed, what failed on the way, and what is still open.

Open items

  • Upgrade Next 15 to Next 16 to clear the two accepted dependency advisories (A and B). Tracked in ADR-006.
  • Application-level field encryption for evidence blobs is a tracked follow-up. Column and volume encryption is delegated to production infrastructure (SEC-10).
  • Scope verification accepts a manual, audited attestation as well as DNS TXT proof. When attested, the DNS proof is decorative (SEC-7).
  • Add Semgrep, Gitleaks and Trivy to CI. They were not run during the build (security audit advisory).
  • Cloud swap: the AWS managed services and a production Resend key were not provisioned in the build. The swap is documented in QUICKSTART (ADR-008).
