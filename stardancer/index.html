Open items

  • Not verified automatically: live Stripe billing and the load-test targets. Most load targets were missed on the build machine, where its 4 shared CPUs saturate. Single requests are within target: graph reads at 2,000 entities take 0.1 to 0.5 s. Generating demo data went from 11 to 15 s down to 3.2 s in 0.1.7.
  • Web unit-test coverage is about 93% of statements, up from about 63% in 0.1.6. The new tests found two small bugs, both fixed. The server's coverage is about 87%.
  • HSTS is on. On localhost it lasts one day, so other local projects on the same ports are forced onto HTTPS for that day.
